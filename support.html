Frequently asked questions

How do I open Parent Settings?
On the home screen, press and hold the lock button for 3 seconds.

There's no sound on iPhone / iPad.
Turn the volume up with the side buttons. Also check Parent Settings to make sure Sound and Voice prompts are switched on.

How do I stop my child from leaving the app?
Use Guided Access on iPhone/iPad (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access) or App pinning on Android (Settings → Security → App pinning).

Does the app need internet, show ads or have purchases?
No. Bobo Play works fully offline, has no ads and no in-app purchases. All games are included.

How do I reset progress?
Parent Settings → Reset Progress.
