Privacy Policy

Bobo Play: Baby & Toddler Games
HomePrivacy PolicySupport

Effective date: October 5, 2026

In short: Bobo Play does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The app works fully offline.

1. Who we are

Bobo Play ("the app") is developed and published by Barma Health Innovation Group SARL ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information on the Apple App Store and Google Play versions of the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information or usage data. Specifically, the app:

  • does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photo, voice recording or any other personal information;
  • does not require or offer an account or login;
  • does not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs;
  • does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo library;
  • does not use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and does not track users across apps or websites;
  • does not send any data to us or to any third party — it has no backend server.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences locally on the device so it remembers your choices — for example sound, music and voice-prompt settings, language and game progress (stars). This information never leaves the device, is not accessible to us, and can be erased at any time from Parent Settings → Reset Progress or by uninstalling the app.

4. Spoken prompts (text-to-speech)

To help children who cannot read yet, the app speaks short instructions such as "Find the cat!" using the text-to-speech engine built into your device's operating system. The text spoken is fixed content from the app; no information about the child is used or sent. Voice prompts can be turned off in Parent Settings.

5. Children's privacy

Bobo Play is designed for babies and toddlers (about 6 months to 3 years) and is intended to be used with a parent or caregiver. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, it is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR), Apple's App Store Kids Category guidelines and Google Play's Families Policy.

  • No third-party advertising or analytics.
  • No external links, social features, chat or purchases available to children.
  • Parent Settings are protected by a parental gate (press and hold for 3 seconds).

6. Permissions

The app does not request any runtime permissions. On Android, the app package includes the standard network permission required by the app framework; the app itself does not connect to the internet. Light vibration (haptic feedback) may be used on taps on supported devices.

7. Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party services that collect data. App downloads and updates are handled by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies.

8. Data sharing and sale

Since we do not collect any data, we do not share, transfer or sell any data to anyone.

9. Data security and retention

No personal data is collected or transmitted, so none is retained by us. Preferences stored on the device remain there until you reset them or uninstall the app.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to provide or delete — but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app ever handled data differently, we would update this policy before releasing it.
